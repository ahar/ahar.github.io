## Problem

Tracked platforms operating in off-road conditions — jungles, rocky terrain, slopes, trenches, and adverse weather (heavy rain, fog, dust storms) — require autonomy stacks far beyond structured indoor AMR navigation.

## Approach

As **team lead**, I led development of the first off-road autonomous navigation system (ANS) for a tracked platform with **DRDO**:

- **Multi-sensor fusion** — LiDAR, EO/IR camera, IMU
- **Deep learning** object detection for obstacle recognition
- **2.5D elevation mapping** for traversability and negative obstacle avoidance
- **Model predictive control** for motion on uneven terrain
- **ROS 2** software framework for modular integration and testing

## Results

- Project **completed by August 2025**
- First off-road ANS of its kind for a tracked platform in India
- Validated across jungle, rocky, slope, trench, and desert conditions with adverse weather

## Technologies

ROS 2 · LiDAR · EO/IR · IMU · Deep learning · Elevation mapping · MPC · Sim2Real
